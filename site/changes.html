Changes

What's new on the D&D Toolbox home page. Each tool keeps its own change log, linked from its header.

November 13, 2026

  • Shared characters. A new Characters section lists your characters. Each one links into every tool that can use it, and a switcher in the header of every site picks the active character. A Paladin built in the Multiclass Planner can carry its spell list and level into the Spell Planner, and a druid can carry its level and circle into Wild Shape.
  • Spell Planner. A new tool for picking and ranking spells from any class or subclass list. It has a card here, and every site's footer links to it.
  • Update notice. If the site is updated while you have it open, a small bar offers to reload so you get the new version.
  • Multiclass Planner. The planner is live, so its card now opens it, and every site's footer links to it.
  • Consistent design. The change log moved to its own page, and the header, footer and theme are now shared across the D&D Toolbox sites. Choosing light or dark on one site now carries over to the others.
  • Launch. A home page linking the Wild Shape Beast List and the Item Finder, with the Multiclass Planner coming soon.
