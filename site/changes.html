Changes

What's new on the D&D Toolbox home page. Each tool keeps its own change log, linked from its header.

November 19, 2026

  • Weapon attacks. The Character Sheet has an Attacks card: weapons from an imported character show their to-hit and damage (with ability, proficiency and +1/+2/+3 bonuses, and the two-handed damage of versatile weapons), plus an unarmed strike. Add your own attacks too. Tap any number to roll it.
  • Plural item names link. Text such as bags of holding or arrows of dragon slaying now links to the Item Finder like the singular name does.
  • Dice rolling. On the Character Sheet, tap initiative, an ability, a save, a spell attack or a hit die to roll it. Stat blocks in every tool roll the same way, including attacks and damage. d20 rolls show the advantage and disadvantage results too, damage can be re-rolled as a critical hit, and the last 30 rolls are kept in one log shared by every tool. The Dice roller button on the home page opens it any time.
  • Links between tools. Spell and magic item names, conditions such as prone, and creature types now link to the tool that explains them: the Spell Planner, the Item Finder, the Rules Reference and Monster Lookup. On the Character Sheet, your spells link to the Spell Planner.
  • Encounter Builder. A new card in Run the game, and in every page's Run menu, opens the encounter builder in Monster Lookup: pick monsters for your party and see how hard the fight is by the 2014 or 2024 rules.
  • Search every tool. One search box on the home page finds spells, items, monsters, feats, origins, rules and Wild Shape beasts across all the tools and takes you straight to the entry. Press / to jump to it. Tools join the search as they publish their lists.
  • Works offline. Every page now keeps working with no signal after you've opened it once, and Save every tool for offline use on the home page saves all of them in one go. Phones can add the toolbox to the home screen as an app, with its own icon.
  • Link previews. Links shared in Discord or a message now show a preview card for each tool.
  • Characters page. Characters moved from the bottom of the home page to their own Characters page, with a full editor (up to three classes with subclasses, and ability scores by point buy, standard array or by hand), Share links and backups. On the home page, your saved characters now sit right under Pick a tool, and a Quick build card starts the Build a character group.
  • Import a character. Bring in a character from D&D Beyond (link or pasted data), a Foundry VTT export, a Fight Club 5 XML file or DiceCloud. You see a preview first; only names and numbers are kept, and files never leave your browser.

November 18, 2026

  • New layout. Tools are grouped into Build a character, At the table and Run the game, with quick links at the top.
  • Three new tools. Feats & Origins (feats, species and backgrounds, with what your character qualifies for), Rules Reference (48 table rules with what changed in 2024) and Monster Lookup (every SRD creature with full stat blocks). All three are in the footer of every site.
  • Character Sheet and Party. The Character Sheet pulls each character's build, spells, Wild Shape forms and feats from the other tools into one page you can track HP, slots and resources on at the table. The Party page shows the whole group, including players' characters added from their share links.
  • Motion and charts. Cards ease in as you scroll, and the tools are getting small charts with a hover readout, arrow-key reading and a table view. Turn on your system's reduced-motion setting to switch the animation off.

November 17, 2026

  • Multiclass Planner: Druid and Barbarian, up to three classes. Builds can now mix up to three classes, and Druid and Barbarian are in, for 2014 and 2024 rules. A new rules note explains why Rage and other resistances don't stack.
  • Multiclass Planner: Bard, Cleric, Wizard, Ranger and Artificer.
  • Multiclass Planner: all 13 classes. Fighter (with Eldritch Knight), Rogue (with Arcane Trickster) and Monk are in, and the D&D Beyond import accepts every class.

November 15, 2026

  • Character share links. The Share button next to the character switcher, on every site, copies a link that carries the character. Whoever opens it, on any device, is asked whether to add it to their characters. The hub's character cards have a Share link too.

November 13, 2026

  • Shared characters. A new Characters section lists your characters. Each one links into every tool that can use it, and a switcher in the header of every site picks the active character. A Paladin built in the Multiclass Planner can carry its spell list and level into the Spell Planner, and a druid can carry its level and circle into Wild Shape.
  • Spell Planner. A new tool for picking and ranking spells from any class or subclass list. It has a card here, and every site's footer links to it.
  • Update notice. If the site is updated while you have it open, a small bar offers to reload so you get the new version.
  • Multiclass Planner. The planner is live, so its card now opens it, and every site's footer links to it.
  • Consistent design. The change log moved to its own page, and the header, footer and theme are now shared across the D&D Toolbox sites. Choosing light or dark on one site now carries over to the others.
  • Launch. A home page linking the Wild Shape Beast List and the Item Finder, with the Multiclass Planner coming soon.
