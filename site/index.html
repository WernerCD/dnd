      Multiclass Planner

      Plan a multiclass build of up to three classes, level by level, for 2014 or 2024 rules: spell slots, Pact Magic, features, resources and milestones.

      • 2014 and 2024 rules
      • Pact slots kept separate from spell slots
      • Presets and shareable build links
      Open Planner →

      Spell Planner

      Browse every class and subclass spell list, then pick and rank the spells you want for a character.

      • 2014 and 2024 rules
      • Pick, rank and reorder spells
      • Shareable spell list links
      Open Spell Planner →

      Feats & Origins

      Feats, species and backgrounds for 2014 and 2024 in one searchable place, with what your character qualifies for now or at a planned level.

      • 296 feats, 209 species, 161 backgrounds
      • Full SRD text; facts and summaries for the rest
      • Qualifies check, Taken and Planned marks
      Open Feats & Origins →

      At the table

      Look things up fast while you play.

      Wild Shape Beast List

      Every beast a druid can become, filtered by level for standard and Circle of the Moon druids.

      • Full SRD stat blocks in both editions
      • Damage per round and feature filters
      • Star your known forms
      Open Wild Shape →

      Item Finder

      Magic items and gear, searchable by type, rarity, attunement and source book.

      • Full SRD 5.1 and 5.2 text
      • Index of items from later books
      • Filters saved in the link
      Open Item Finder →

      Character Sheet

      A one-page sheet for the table, built from your plans in every tool, with HP, slots and resources you can tick off as you play.

      • HP, temp HP, death saves, hit dice, slot and resource pips
      • Conditions, exhaustion, concentration; short and long rest with undo
      • Prints as a clean paper sheet; built for phones
      Open Character Sheet →

      Rules Reference

      A fast, searchable quick reference for the table: conditions, actions, grapple and shove, cover, resistance, death saves, resting, concentration and more.

      • 48 topics, both editions
      • What changed in 2024 on every rule that differs
      • A link per rule, phone-friendly, prints as a cheat sheet
      Open Rules Reference →

      Monster Lookup

      Every creature in the free SRDs, 325 from 2014 and 331 from 2024, with full stat blocks and filters for CR, type, size, defences and movement.

      • Every SRD 5.1 and 5.2 creature, full stat blocks
      • Filters for CR, type, defences, movement, legendary
      • Stars, shareable links, creatures-by-CR chart
      Open Monster Lookup →

      Encounter Builder

      Pick monsters for your party and see how hard the fight is, by the 2014 or the 2024 rules.

      • Uses the party from the Party page, or any levels you type
      • XP totals and difficulty for 2014 and 2024
      • Share, print with stat blocks, works offline
      Build an encounter →
