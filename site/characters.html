Characters

One list of characters for every D&D Toolbox tool

Your characters are shared by every tool. A Paladin built in the Multiclass Planner opens in the Spell Planner with its spell list, level and spellcasting modifier already set, and a druid's level and circle carry over to Wild Shape. Characters are stored only in this browser: use a character's Share link to send it to another device or player, or Export to back them all up.

    Import from another site

    Bring in a character you already have elsewhere. Pick where it comes from:

    1. On D&D Beyond, make the character public (the character's Settings, then Privacy: Public), or skip to step 3.
    2. Paste its link (dndbeyond.com/characters/…) or number, then Fetch.

    3. Or paste the character data

    Open the character data in a new tab (you'll see a page of text; signed in to D&D Beyond, this also works for private characters), select all, copy, and paste it here.

    In Foundry, open the Actors tab, right-click the character and choose Export Data. Then pick the .json file. Works with the dnd5e system (versions 3 to 5).

    In Fight Club 5 or Game Master 5, open the character and export it as XML (share or export menu). Then pick the .xml file.

    DiceCloud v2: export the character's JSON (or open dicecloud.com/api/creature/<id> while signed in and save the page), then pick the file.

      What is imported, and what stays private
      • Imported: name, rules edition, classes with levels and subclasses, ability scores, max HP, AC, speed, initiative, saving throw and skill proficiencies, passive scores, resistances and immunities, species and background, and the names of feats, spells and items.
      • Not imported: descriptions, rules text, notes, portraits, homebrew text, current HP or spent slots. The tools use their own data for what a feat or spell does.
      • Private: files and pasted text are read in this browser only and are never uploaded. The one request this page makes is the D&D Beyond fetch itself, straight from your browser to D&D Beyond.
      • The other tools can then offer to use the imported spells, feats and items; the Character Sheet shows the imported AC, speed, skills and passives.
